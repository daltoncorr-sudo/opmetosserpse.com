About Opmet Osserpse

HomeHome

Opmet Osserpse is a creative studio founded in Venice, California.

We bring together innovative thinkers to create incredible work with our clients.

We are designers, musicians, filmmakers, writers, developers, artists.

Our work starts by hand, with pencils, paper and physical tools.

Our music and sound studio is called espresso tempo.
