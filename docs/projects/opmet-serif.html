The wink

The O’s stress, from upright

Classic stress tilts the thin parts of a bowl to the left, toward 11 and 5 o’clock. Opmet Serif tilts them to the right, toward 1 and 7, on the O, the e, the p and every letter with a bowl. The studio’s name runs backward, and so, slightly, do its letters.

Drawn as strokes

The g, Text Regular

Each letter is built from a few pen strokes, each a line of points with a heading and a width, at three weights and two sizes. The outline comes last, and every weight between is its interpolation.
