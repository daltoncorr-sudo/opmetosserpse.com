HDtracks

HDtracks sells high-resolution music to people who care how a recording sounds. In 2019 the store was rebranded and rebuilt from the ground up, from the database to the logo, so a deep catalog reads as clearly as it sounds. Around it runs a year-round line of campaigns and email. Retention and sales rose.
