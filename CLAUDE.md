# Working on opmetosserpse.com

Read `README.md` first. D (Dalton Corr) is the creative director and makes every taste, money, legal and irreversible call.

## Hard rules
- **Never push, publish, deploy, buy, sign or send anything.** Commit locally only. Hand D the exact command or click instead.
- **Never change the words** in `content/` except to fix a typo, and say which. D approved them.
- **Keep the restraint, to a top-agency standard:** no menu bar; the cover is only the coins and the name (three carved stone coins in the hand's slot, D, Oct. 15; the hand's moves stay in `moves/`, unused); image first; very few words; plain lists of names; no section headers; off-white ground; two faces, Opmet Serif and Instrument Sans; text at one weight, the masthead in Opmet Serif Black (D, Oct. 13, 2026); project and Journal titles large but regular, with the project's role under the title, small, in the serif italic (D, Oct. 20); the cover's name and About's text Opmet Serif regular (D, Oct. 15); only buttons, and About's links, are italic (D, Oct. 13; About's links italic and not underlined, D, Oct. 16); one 0.5-second motion value (the exceptions: the coins drop, and can be picked up and thrown, on the cover and beside the work list's last name, `static/js/coins.js`; the About rings were removed, D, Oct. 16). No new UI elements, cards, icons, badges or color (one exception, D, Oct. 19: Sunny's Bookshop sets four chapters in rounded panels, three on a light off-white and Journal & Press on its Brand Bible's Ink Navy; nowhere else).
- **Project pages follow their own template** (see "A project page" in `README.md`): at the top, the studio's small nameplate (Opmet Osserpse in Opmet Serif Bold, no coin, D, Oct. 15, linking to the cover; D, Oct. 13 and 15) with Work and Home under it (Home, D, Oct. 9; Work, not All work, back to the list as the visitor left it, D, Oct. 15); the title large in the serif, regular (D, Oct. 20), the focus of the page; the role under it, small, in the serif italic; the facts and credits under that; then the images, films and 3D pieces of the project's daltoncorr.com page, in its order (pages D changed on purpose list their media in their notes file instead: HDtracks, Sunny's Bookshop, HollyShorts Comedy 10, Don't Let Them Out and Chop Suey Club, whose daltoncorr.com page has no pictures: its logo, from D, as a cut-out; check.py holds every page to `_review/2026-10-19_media-order-record_v08.json`), large and close together; Instrument Sans with its italic for the rest, in two sizes; up to four short chapter labels, in the left margin from 1100 px only (Sunny's Bookshop, built after its Brand Bible: one per section, up to ten, shown above each section at every width, D, Oct. 19); no notes (Sunny's Bookshop alone has small labels under its marks, after its Brand Bible, with numerals in its Gilt, #CBA65E: D's color exception, with its panels' grounds; its rows of marks are level plates, pictures on one line and labels on the next; Red Mask has each poster's year and period research under it, D, Oct. 14); Work again at the end, with Home and Back to top beside it (D, Oct. 9). Never Back or a next project. Sunny's type specimens and stationery are images made once from the package's fonts and the Bible's page source; the fonts themselves never ship. Films play once and rest, muted, even those with sound (D, Oct. 10); a film with sound has Play with sound right under it, which becomes Mute. None loops, except two D chose: Sunny's Bookshop's signature film (sb-signature-script), which opens its page, and HollyShorts Comedy 10's animated poster (hollyshorts-comedy-10-poster-popcorn); check.py allows only those two.
- **Opmet Serif is a finished page** (`content/pages/`, D, Oct. 14): D's own design, with its own styles, motion and interactive pieces, inside the project frame every page has: the nameplate with Work and Home, and at the end Work, Home and Back to top, in the controls' italic; it slides down from home like the rest (D, Oct. 14). Its content is D's file, brought in by `python3 pages.py opmet-serif <D's page>`; change it only that way, with D's next version. It is third in Selected work. It has no captions, readouts or visible section titles (D, Oct. 15): the headings stay in the page for screen readers only, and the demos' readouts stay hidden because its script writes into them. Version 12 (D, Oct. 20, `04_Site/2026-10-20_opmet-serif-case-study_v12/`): the title regular with the role under it, the hand cut, the alphabet first across the page (lowercase left, capitals right), then the wink, the strokes, the weights and italics and the features in use (each line named, small, in the sans: a specimen needs it), the italic and name boards, the tester, In use with five staged pieces (real type, rendered, never generated) and Proofs and process.
- Dalton Corr's name doesn't appear on the site, except in a project's credits, as "Dalton Corr, Opmet Osserpse" (Sunny's Bookshop: "Creative direction: Dalton Corr" alone, and no photography credit; D, Oct. 13). Opmet Serif's page is the one other place: its quote is credited "Dalton, Opmet Osserpse founder" (D, Oct. 14); the handwriting under it reads "opmet osserpse", in D's hand (D, Oct. 15).
- No analytics, cookies, trackers or third-party embeds. The privacy page promises none.
- No frameworks, bundlers or npm. Python 3 and Pillow only, plus ffmpeg for video and three.js (r128, served from the site) for the 3D pieces. The stone coins are D's one exception (Oct. 15): three.js 0.169.0 and cannon-es 0.20.0 in `static/js/vendor/coins/`, served from the site; upgrade them in a new folder, never over these files (they cache a year).
- "AI" never appears on the site except inside Journal article bodies, where the studio may write about it. `check.py` skips only `.article-body` for that word (and for all caps); anywhere else it fails.
- daltoncorr.com stays live as it is. Never change, redirect or push anything in the daltoncorr repo; the clone in `_sources/` is read-only, for images.
- Be light on usage: no exploratory browsing; check pages with the local server and `check.py`.

## Commands
```bash
python3 build.py --daltoncorr ../_sources/daltoncorr-porfolio   # build into docs/
python3 check.py                                                # must print "All clear"
python3 serve.py                                                # preview on :8080
python3 moves.py                                                # the hand's moves: on, off, problems
python3 moves_lab.py                                            # workbench for the moves: _lab/hand.html
```

The hand's moves on the cover live in `moves/`, one folder each. Read `moves/README.md` before touching them.
